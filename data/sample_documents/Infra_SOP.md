# Infrastructure Document

## Topic 15
All primary production workloads must be deployed in the us-east-1 AWS region.

## Topic 16
Automated database backups are retained for 35 days in S3 Glacier.

## Topic 17
P1 (Critical) incidents have a strict 15-minute response SLA and a 2-hour resolution target.

## Topic 18
Emergency deployments require explicit approval from the VP of Engineering or the on-call Director.

## Topic 19
Terraform is the exclusive tool for provisioning AWS infrastructure resources.
