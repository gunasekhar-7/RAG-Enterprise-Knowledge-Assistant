# Travel Policy Document

## Topic 10
The daily per diem for meals and incidental expenses in London is $125 USD.

## Topic 11
First-class flights are only permitted for international travel exceeding 8 hours of continuous flight time.

## Topic 12
All travel expenses must be submitted via Concur within 14 days of trip completion.

## Topic 13
Alcohol is only reimbursable during official client dinners, capped at 2 drinks per person.

## Topic 14
The company reimburses the cost of TSA PreCheck or Global Entry for frequent travelers (3+ trips/year).
